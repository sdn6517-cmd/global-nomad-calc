Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Nomad Number Lab is a set of free calculators for people who work across borders. This page explains, in plain language, what happens to information when you use it. The short version: the calculators run inside your own browser, and we do not collect what you type.

What we do not collect

  • No accounts. There is no sign-up, no login and no newsletter form.
  • No calculator input is uploaded. Token counts, salary targets, tax rates, cities and dates are processed by JavaScript on your device. They never reach a server we control.
  • No tracking pixels or analytics scripts. The page loads no third-party analytics.
  • No profile. We do not build or buy advertising profiles of readers.

What stays in your browser

The values you enter are written into the address bar of the page (a query string such as ?target=90000&tax=25) so that a scenario can be bookmarked or shared. That URL is visible to your browser history and to anyone you send it to. We do not store it. Clearing the query string returns the page to its defaults.

We do not use localStorage or cookies of our own.

Advertising and third-party cookies

This site is funded by advertising — Google AdSense and, potentially, other third-party networks. Ad slots are reserved at the top, inside the articles and at the bottom of each page. When advertising is live, third-party vendors — including Google — may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the internet.
  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or opt out of third-party vendor cookies at aboutads.info and youronlinechoices.com.
  • For readers in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, a consent message is shown before advertising cookies are set, and you can change or withdraw that choice at any time from the link in the consent notice.
  • Advertising vendors operate under their own privacy policies. We do not receive the personal data they process, and we do not pass your calculator inputs to them.

Server logs

The site is served as static files by a hosting provider. Like every web server, that provider may record standard technical logs — IP address, user agent, requested file, timestamp — for security and reliability. We do not combine those logs with anything else and do not use them to identify readers.

Your rights

Because we do not hold personal data about readers, there is normally nothing for us to export or delete. If you are in the EEA or UK (GDPR) or California (CCPA/CPRA), you still have the right to ask what personal data is held, to have it corrected or deleted, and to object to processing. Requests can be sent to the address below and will be answered within 30 days. For data held by advertising partners, use the opt-out links above or contact those partners directly.

Children

The site is intended for people managing their own work and income. It is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Links to other sites

Articles link to vendor pricing pages and other resources. Once you follow an external link, the privacy policy of that site applies.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of the page changes with it. Material changes — for example adding analytics or a newsletter — will be described in the change note rather than made silently.

Contact

Questions about this policy or a data request: hello@nomadnumberlab.com. We answer in English or Korean.

This policy describes the site as published. It is not legal advice; if you operate a similar site, have your own policy reviewed for the jurisdictions you serve.
